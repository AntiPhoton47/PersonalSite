The towering mountains enclosing Innsbruck were looking particularly impressive today as my colleague and I prepared to welcome a group of aspiring scientists (high school students) from Italy to demonstrate our outreach booth. 

This was the first time we have done a somewhat private event as we normally do university-wide events, and we were both a bit nervous; high school students are notoriously difficult to engage with on average simply because they do not show much enthusiasm, and our booth needs engagement to work at all.

We set up the room into little stations and tried to design the layout so that there was good flow between each station. Once they arrived, I did the introduction and it was off to the races! There were some clear favourites with the image generation station and the reinforcement learning game, the latter of which was occupied by a group of boys having great fun and really putting the features of the game to the test. These boys seemed genuinely curious about how reinforcement learning worked and their teachers nearby made sure my explanations connected with them.

Unfortunately I had to leave before the end of the event but what I saw during my time was inspiring. I have to thank the teachers and some brave students for their feedback on the booth, which led to some really nice improvements like greater linguistic accessibility through having the reinforcement learning game interface translated into multiple languages.

I cannot wait for the next event!
